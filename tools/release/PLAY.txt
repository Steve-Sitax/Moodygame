SCHELDEMIST - Antwerp, autumn 1873
==================================

HOW TO PLAY

1. Start the game:
   - Windows: double-click "Start Scheldemist.bat".
     If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway".
   - Mac: double-click "Start Scheldemist.command".
     If the Mac says it cannot open it: open System Settings, then Privacy & Security.
     Scroll down and click "Open Anyway". Then double-click the file again.
   - Linux: run ./start-scheldemist.sh in a terminal.

2. A black window opens. Then the game opens in your web browser.
   Keep the black window open while you play. Close it to stop the game.

3. Use Chrome for the best speed.

You need nothing else. Node (the program that runs the game) is inside this folder.


THE TOWN'S VOICE (AI)

The game works without AI. The townspeople then use written lines.
To let an AI speak for the town, open the menu (Esc), then "AI setup". Choose one:
  - Claude, with your Claude login: install Claude Code, run "claude" once and log in.
  - Claude, with an API key from console.anthropic.com (you pay per call).
  - Codex, with your ChatGPT login: install Codex and log in.
  - Ollama: a free AI that runs on your own computer (https://ollama.com).
  - Any OpenAI-compatible server, with its address and key.
  - No AI: just walk around.
Use the "Test" button to see if your choice works.


YOUR SAVE

Your save and settings are in the "data" folder next to this file.
To move to a new version: copy the "data" folder into the new version's folder.


PLAY TOGETHER

Play together is off. To let people in your house join, open the menu, then "Together".
