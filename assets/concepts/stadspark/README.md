# Stadspark reference and foliage

Original images generated on 2026-09-29 with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool. No third-party reference images. `park-examples.png` is a concept reference, not a game screenshot. `foliage-source.png` is the production RGBA source.

`python tools/textures/stadspark_atlas.py` packs the eight cells into `tools/blender/art/stadspark_foliage.png` (eight 64-pixel cutouts with transparent margins). `tools/blender/build_park_plants.py` embeds the strip in `client/public/models/park_plants.glb`. The new foliage is used by the park's elm, plane, lime, maple, willow, rhododendron, holly and flower meshes. Existing reeds, lilies and bark retain their attributed sources.

The animal meshes are original geometry made by `tools/blender/build_park_animals.py`, with vertex-coloured feathers and fur and shared animated wings. No downloaded models or new packages.

## Concept prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original environment concept reference sheet for Scheldemist, a low-poly first-person game in Antwerp autumn 1873. Two large side-by-side examples of an improved Stadspark: left a clear overcast afternoon at eye level beside a curving pond and delicate white iron suspension footbridge; right the same park after rain with gaslamps and cloudy sky reflected in rippling water. English landscape garden within a tall old brick rampart, layered golden elm and lime crowns with believable branching trunks, copper maples, drooping willows, evergreen shrubs, late muted purple asters and cream chrysanthemums, reeds and muddy leaf-strewn gravel paths. Ducks and swans reflected in water, squirrel climbing a trunk, respectable Victorian couple walking a dog, a keeper with broom and basket. Grounded realistic proportions, handcrafted angular 3D meshes with small detailed painted textures, grey-green water and restrained autumn colors, moody but legible. Dense naturalistic planting frames open views of the pond; avoid spherical lollipop trees, modern objects, fantasy, glossy plastic, text, watermark. This is a modeling reference, not a claim of an actual screenshot.

## Production foliage prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production foliage alpha texture atlas for low-poly 3D trees and bushes, original assets. Exactly 4 columns by 2 rows of eight separate branch clusters on genuinely transparent background, equal square cells, generous transparent margins, no text no borders no shadows. Order left to right top row: dense golden elm branch spray with many small serrated leaves; copper orange plane branch with large lobed leaves; yellow-green lime branch with heart-shaped leaves; long hanging fine willow foliage. Bottom row: dark olive evergreen rhododendron leaf spray; glossy small holly leaves with a few dark red berries; copper red maple branch spray; low clump of muted purple asters and cream chrysanthemums on green stems. Each cluster fills about 85 percent of its cell, naturally overlapping leaves with a few small transparent holes; branching twigs visible, stems attach at bottom center. Hand painted naturalistic diffuse albedo, neutral even lighting, crisp distinct leaf silhouettes, no ambient cast shadow, no 3D presentation, no pots, no background, no color swatches, no labels. Autumn Antwerp 1873 muted earthy colors but clear midtones, avoid black leaves. These eight images will be downsampled into 64 pixel cutout textures and mapped to modeled branches.
