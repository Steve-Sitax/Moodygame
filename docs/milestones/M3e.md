## What is in
| Part | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Residents | `server/src/town/population.ts`, `places.ts` | About 190 people made once per new game by the engine (never by a model), from one seed: plain Flemish names, age, sex, households (couples, children, widows, lodgers, three street children in a cellar), a home at a real house door (`walkmap.ts` reads `shared/city_build.json` and the walk map, only doors you can walk to), a trade (docker, natie man, porter, carter, boatman, sailor, fishwife, market woman, baker, grocer, chandler, tobacconist, pawnbroker, cobbler, draper, publican, clerk, merchant, maid, laundress, seamstress, housewife, police agent, priest, sexton, lamplighter, beggar, pickpocket, children, errand boys), a workplace, stats 0-10 (honesty, temper, piety, warmth, greed, courage, gossip, wealth) biased by trade, a model, and sometimes a dog. |
| Schedules | `server/src/town/schedule.ts` (pure; the client imports the same file) | A working day and a Sunday per person: work hours, dinner at home or the tavern, errands at the market, children at play, evenings at the tavern door, mass on Sunday for the pious. Night people: pickpockets 21:00-4:00, half the police on the night beat, sailors in the taverns till late, publicans till 2:00, the lamplighter at dusk and dawn. |
| Save | `server/src/town/store.ts`, `db.ts` | New table `resident`; every resident is also an `npc` row, so relationships, memories and gossip reuse M3. `migrate()` adds the rumour columns; `ensureTown()` gives an older save a town without touching the player; `repairTown()` mends a town from an older build in place. `node scripts/rebuild-town.ts <save>` makes a new town for a save (with a backup). |
| The town in the street | `client/src/game/town.ts`, small puppet API in `crowd.ts` | Everyone runs by schedule, cheaply: where they should be now, and a walk there out of sight. Near Jef (55 m, at most 34 at once) they become people of the crowd, walked on its grid (A*, stuck checks, keep right, round Jef): they step out of their door, walk to work, sell at the stall, carry sacks from the quay to the door, walk the beat, stand at the tavern door taking turns to talk, walk home and go in. The nameless crowd stays home when the town is loaded. |
| Children | `town.ts` `play()` | Children out to play go to where the others are (they look for each other), then play tag: "it" chases the nearest, the others run, a tag passes it on (no tagging back straight away). |
| Dogs and cats | `tools/blender/build_animals.py` -> `animals.glb`, `client/src/game/animals.ts` | Four dogs and four cats, rigged, with idle, walk, run, sit, lie, sniff. Some townspeople's dogs follow them at heel; a few strays trot about and come to sniff Jef; cats sit on doorsteps and run from dogs, and from Jef when he comes right up. |
| Stalls and shops | `tools/blender/build_stalls.py` -> `stalls.glb`, `client/src/game/stalls.ts` | Six fish stalls on the Vismarkt, six stalls on the Grote Markt, and shop fronts (table and awning beside the door) for the bakers, grocers, chandler, tobacconist, cobbler and draper. The keeper stands behind. When the keeper goes home the awning is rolled up and a tarred tarpaulin is tied over the goods. |
| Shopping | `server/src/trade.ts` | Townspeople sell by stall or trade at engine prices: herring 5 c and eel 12 c from the fishwives, rye bread 6 c, an apple 2 c, ship's biscuit 4 c, a pot of beer 5 c (drunk on the spot) and jenever 10 c at the tavern door. Only while they are at work: "the shop is shut" after hours. |
| Thieves | `server/src/town/thieves.ts`, `town.ts` | After 20:00 a pickpocket out on his haunts who sees Jef alone comes up behind him. The engine decides: whether the hand gets in, how much (20-45% of what Jef has, at most 60 c), once per thief and at most twice a night. For 25 s Jef can grab him (E "grab ...!") and get it back. Both are remembered and become rumours. |
| Lights for work | `town.ts` `postEmployers()`, `people.ts` | After dark, anyone with work open for Jef shows it: the board's townspeople move to the nearest gas lamp by their post (or carry a lantern if there is none), Sooi, the widow, Tuur and Fientje hold a lantern. Police and the lamplighter carry lanterns, and some others do too. |
| Work at every quay | `hooks/jobBoard.ts`, `shared/spots.json` (15 new spots) | Five new employers, townspeople with fixed ids: the Katoennatie foreman at the Entrepot (naties), a fish merchant at the Vismarkt (burgerij), the sergeant of the water police at the Werf (politie), a brewer on the Canal des Brasseurs (burgerij), the sexton of the cathedral (kerk). They stand at their posts by day (`people.ts`), W in talk takes their work. The board has 4-7 jobs, at least two away from the Rijnkaai. The engine keeps each employer's work on their own places, a carry at most 70 m a trip. Pay stays engine-owned. |
| Talk with everyone | `server/src/town/talk.ts` | E on anyone in the street. The opening line is the engine's, at once, from their stats, trade, the hour and what they have heard of Jef. The first real reply of a meeting goes to Claude with a short prompt (the person, their family, stats, mood, what they are doing, what they know of Jef, the work they know of, their wares) and a small schema; that call also writes their one-line persona, kept for good. Topic answers after that are the engine's (work, rumours, trade, family, the news). At most 3 calls a meeting, 30 a day for the townspeople, never into the last 15 of the day's 80. Late or wrong: engine lines. Typed words: the same gate and fence as M3. |
| Rumours | `server/src/town/rumours.ts` | A memory can carry a gist ("Jef ...", past tense) and a tone (-2 to +2). Engine events write them (lifting goods, a job done honestly or not, a job left undone, strange talk, a pocket picked, a thief caught); Claude may add one from a talk. Every game hour, and three times at night, each holder may tell a fresh rumour to 1-3 people of their circle (household, workmates, neighbours within 30 m; Sooi, the widow, Tuur and Fientje to the people working near them) by their gossip stat; each telling is weaker, below 3 it stops, nobody hears the same rumour twice. People greet Jef by what they heard ("You're the one who lifted Sooi's goods, aren't you?") and their mood follows it. |
| Path check | `main.ts` `paths()` | Also checks every home door, work door, haul end, post, stall front and seller spot of the town, and the new spots. |

## Not yet
- Families walk out together only by sharing places (a Sunday stroll, the same tavern); nobody leads a family procession yet.
- Townspeople do not look at Jef while he walks past; they face him only in talk.
- A townsperson cut off by a wall far out of sight is simply moved to where they should be.
- Cats sit on doorsteps and quay edges; windowsills and wall tops need wall-top data from the city (not in the walk map).
- Night life is thin after 22:00: thieves, night police, publicans and sailors.
