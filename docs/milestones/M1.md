# M1 - Fog walk, 2026-09-23

Goal: walk the Rijnkaai in first person in the browser. Retro PS1 look, thick fog, gas lamps, water and foghorn sound. No AI, no server.

## How to run
```
cd D:\Code\MoodyGame\client
npm install
npm run dev
```
Open http://localhost:5173 in Chrome or Edge. Click the paper note. WASD to walk, Shift to hurry, mouse to look, Esc to pause.

## What is in
| Part | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Retro pass | `client/src/retro/retroPass.ts` | Renders at 270 px high (480x270 on 16:9). Nearest upscale. 5-bit colour, Bayer 4x4 dither, light grain, vignette. |
| PS1 material patch | `client/src/retro/psx.ts` | Vertex snap on a half-res grid. Affine texture warp. Linear fog 3-25 m. Warm lamp glow in the fog, computed in closed form per lamp. Water: sine waves, wave normals, lamp reflections. |
| Rijnkaai grey-box | `client/src/world/rijnkaai.ts` | Cobbled quay, quay wall, water, wooden pier, rails. 3 warehouses with stepped gables (KATOENNATIE, HESSENATIE, WED. PEETERS). 1 crane. 6 gas lamps, one sputters. 2 ships (a three-master alongside, a steamer further out) that ride the tide. Crates, barrels, sacks, cart, bollards, rope coils, fences. |
| Textures | `client/src/world/textures.ts` | 64x64, painted in code. Placeholders. |
| First person | `client/src/player/firstPerson.ts` | Pointer lock, WASD, 1.55 m/s walk, 2.9 m/s hurry. Heavy start and stop. Head bob by distance walked. Slight view lag. Collision with props and the quay edge. FOV 75. |
| Sound | `client/src/audio/soundscape.ts` | Recorded, CC0: footsteps (Kenney, stone and wood) and gulls (BigSoundBank, slices of a harbour recording every 12-35 s, far off and muffled). In code: water bed and lapping at the nearest edge, wind, gas hiss per lamp, foghorn every 40-90 s (first one after 9-16 s), rope creak from the ships. Positional audio. |

## Checks done in the browser (2026-09-23)
- `npm run dev` serves the page. `npm run build` passes (only a chunk-size warning for three.js).
- Walked by scripted key presses: along the quay, into a warehouse wall, off the quay edge, onto the pier. Collisions hold. Footsteps change stone -> wood on the pier.
- Audio graph measured with an analyser: beds, foghorn, gulls and both step types give output.
- All 10 footstep samples load.
- Frame rate in the embedded preview: about 50 fps while walking.
- Screenshots from the quay, the pier and the HESSENATIE front match docs/05: cold grey-blue fog, warm only under lamps, dark ships.

Not checked by script: mouse look with a real pointer lock. Steve tests that by hand.

## Changes during the milestone
- First footsteps were synthesized. Steve found them too sharp. Replaced with Kenney "Impact Sounds" (CC0), slowed and low-passed for heavy boots. Synth stays as fallback while samples load.
- Fog colour was converted to linear twice, so the scene was near black. Fixed.
- Lamp glow in the fog was too wide and turned all fog brown. Now tight around each lamp, per docs/05.
- Steve found the synthesized gulls too high. Replaced with BigSoundBank "Gulls on the Harbor" (CC0).
- Steve found textures glitchy up close. Cause: affine warp and vertex snap on 2 m polygons at your feet. Now the warp fades in from 4 to 14 m and the snap from 1.5 to 4 m. Far away it still swims; up close it stays straight.
- Z-fighting (surfaces flickering through each other). The pier deck overlapped the quay wall top by 5 mm: the deck now stops at the wall. Thin details (windows, doors, sills, signs, gable coping, rails, loading dock, edge stones, barrel hoops) use a depth offset so they always win against the wall or ground behind them. Barrel hoops moved 3.5 cm out from the staves.

## Open for later milestones
- Water and wind are still made in code. Steve accepted the foghorn. Swap for recorded CC0 sounds in the M7 audio pass if they do not hold up.
- Rule for new detail meshes: anything that lies within a few cm of a wall or the ground uses a `DECAL` material in `rijnkaai.ts`, or it will flicker.
- Textures are placeholders. The Blender kit replaces them (docs/05 asset plan).
- A dev hook `window.__scheldemist` exists only in `npm run dev`. It is used for scripted checks.
