## Research (sources)
- Fire brigade: Antwerp's fire station stood on the Kipdorp (no. 6, the old House of Portugal) from 1816; the present building is of 1885-88 (Inventaris Onroerend Erfgoed, "Brandweerkazerne", object 5167, https://inventaris.onroerenderfgoed.be/erfgoedobjecten/5167). In the 1870s the Antwerp corps had officers for 106 men but about 78 under arms, with desertion a problem (Weyns, "De Gentse brandweer 1809-1950", https://www.ethesis.net/brandweer/brandweer_deel_II.htm). Antwerp's corps was mainly professional sapeurs-pompiers (Wikipedia FR, "Pompier (Belgique)"). Pumps of the time: hand pumps on hand- or horse-drawn carts, with buckets, hoses and ladders (Wikipedia NL, "Brandspuit"; ETWIE, "Brandweererfgoed in Vlaanderen", https://etwie.be/nl/kennisbank/nieuws/brandweererfgoed-in-vlaanderen-gewaardeerd-deel-1-geschiedenis). The tower watchman's alarm (horn, a flag by day, a lantern by night, then the bell) is documented for Breda, not Antwerp (Klokkenluiders Gilde Breda, "De torenwachters"); the game rings the cathedral's bell. The year of Antwerp's first steam pump, the helmets and whether citizens had to help were not found: the hand pump, the brass helmet and the bucket chain are period-typical, not Antwerp-sourced.
- Naties: 40 to 60 natiebazen owning costly shares (Flanders Today, "The United Nations of Antwerp", https://www.flanderstoday.eu/content/united-nations-antwerp). Foremen (forelieden) hired casual men (natiegasten) each morning; hiring was in the street at the natie warehouse gates, in taverns often kept by the foremen, or at the "Luien hoek"; no central hiring point; wages paid out in the bosses' taverns; casual men found work 2-3 days a week; 3 francs a day in 1898 (Vanfraechem, "The Antwerp docker: militant by nature?", BTNG 31, 2001; Martens, "Personeelsbeleid in de haven van Antwerpen", UGent 2013, https://oudedokken.gilbertus.com/pdf/046.pdf). Hiring offices came only in the 1930s. No source was found for tallymen, chalk or tokens, so the game gives the foreman a book of names, not a tally stick.
- Gas light: the Imperial Continental Gas Association lit Antwerp (Inventaris objects 5259, 5542; Wikipedia EN); lamplighters had their own rounds, a short ladder and a long pole with a flame (a search summary of Het Stille Pand, "Schetsken energie"; the page itself did not load). The number of lamps, lamps per man and the round times are the game's own.
