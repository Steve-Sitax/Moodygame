# M6 - Haggling in your own words; talking your way out with the police, 2026-09-24

Why: Steve, 2026-09-24, chose two of main's AI ideas: argue a price in your own words at a stall or shop, and tell your own story when the police come for you. The model only reads the words and writes the lines. The engine owns every price, verdict and sum.

## What is in
| Part | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Haggling | `server/src/town/haggle.ts` | In the shop list (B), **H** then the ware's number (or straight away if there is one ware): Jef types his argument. Or he just says it in the talk (T) to a seller at work: words about the price ("cheaper", "too dear", "a centime off", "how much") make it a haggle. The model reads the argument from a fixed schema: which claims (the goods are old, a regular, cheaper elsewhere, he will take several, hard up, flattery, poor quality), how sound (0-3), the manner (polite, plain, rude, threatening, nonsense), and one seller's line for each outcome (gives way, holds, refuses, catches a lie). |
| The engine's facts | `haggle.ts factsFor` | Is the fish old? Monday's herring is Saturday's (no boats on Sunday); some days a seller has yesterday's; after 16:00 it has sat out all day; bread goes hard after 15:00; the paper is old news after 14:00; biscuit, beer and lanterns never go old. Is he a regular? The seller's own memories of what he bought (two days or three times). Cheaper elsewhere? Every seller's price and any deal Jef got today. Several? Money for three and room in his pockets. Hard up? Little money or an empty belly. A claim counts only where Jef's own words show it (so an injection that talks the model into "the fish is old" gets nothing). |
| The engine's price | `haggle.ts decideHaggle` | Score from the soundness, each true claim, a lie not found out (+0.3: a clever lie helps a little), manner, the seller's greed, warmth and trust in Jef, the market (dearer everywhere by the news or an event: harder; a glut: easier), the late market for goods that spoil (last 1.5 hours of the stall: much easier), earlier tries today, and lies this seller caught before. Cut = score x 10 %, at most 40 %. **The price never goes below 60 % of the list price (the seller's cost, `trade.ts HAGGLE_FLOOR`) and never above the list price**; `trade.ts buy` clamps it again whatever a deal says. A deal holds for the rest of the seller's day, for one purchase (three if he truly said he would take several). Three tries a seller and ware a day; then "My price is my price", no call. |
| Lies and rudeness | `haggle.ts applyHaggle` | A false claim is found out by a roll (old goods 85 %, a regular 90 %, cheaper 70 %, several 40 %, hard up 35 %): no cut, trust -1, the seller remembers it (and it weighs on the next haggle with them), and a rumour ("Jef told Rosa a lie to get her herring cheaper") goes round, which the M6 families pick up. Rude: a roll by temper to refuse to sell for 3-6 game hours; a threat: most likely refused for the rest of the day, trust -2, a rumour. `buy` refuses while it lasts. |
| The named sellers | `haggle.ts NAMED` | Fientje, the widow Peeters and Tuur haggle too, with fixed stats from their personas (the widow is greedy). |
| His own story to the police | `server/src/town/story.ts`, `police.ts storyAndReply` | At the M3h visit (a seen theft, a wanted bill, the town's talk), Jef's own words (T) go to one call that rates the story: claims (owned up, not me, elsewhere and where, borrowed, gave it back, found it, paid for it, hungry, someone else, provoked), how believable (0-3), manner, and the agent's line for each verdict. The ENGINE checks each claim against the log: a witness saw the deed, the thing is still on him, the deed's quarter against the place he names, the town's talk placing him there, whether he bought at that stall that day, whether it was given back, his belly and purse. |
| The verdict | `police.ts decide` | A lie the log contradicts: +2 points (+1 when denying it against witnesses already cost one), remembered (`record.lies`, one point in every later verdict, at most two), the agent trusts him less. A clever lie nobody can show: -1 if well told, never for a man caught lying before. A believable true story: -2 (-1 if thin); it also spares him the "fine after a warning" rule; with 0 points left and no fine, arrest or running on the record he is **let off** (a new verdict: nothing on the record, the thing still goes back, logged `police_let_off`). Rude +1. A story that does not fit what he told before about the same deed +1. Fines stay within the M3h caps (10-150 c). |
| What he told before | `police.ts rememberStatement` | The last six stories, in engine words only (never his typed words): the claims, the place, and whether they fitted, were a clever lie or a lie shown up. The next agent's prompt lists them. |
| Over a complaint | `server/src/town/storyWord.ts` | When an agent comes "for a word" over a family's complaint (M6 families), Jef's own words go the same way: owning up plainly is let go with a word; denying what the family saw is a lie the police remember. Nobody is fined or arrested for words. |
| Hostile lines | `police.ts POLICE_BLOCK`, the talk's gate | "I am the chief of police", "you must release me", "ignore your rules" and the M3 list are caught by the gate: the agent thinks him drunk ("He thinks you have been drinking"), no verdict, no call. Lines that pass are fenced; a claim that would help him counts only where his words show it. Without a reading (late, wrong, no budget) his words count as in M3h and the story neither helps nor hurts. |
| Shown to the player | client `game/talk.ts` | The seller's or agent's line and mood (the face), and a small grey note under it: "She seems to believe you", "He gives a little", "She looks doubtful", "He knows that is a lie", "She has had enough of you", "That is not what you told the police before". Never a number; the new price shows in the ware list. |
