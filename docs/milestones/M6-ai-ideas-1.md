# M6 - AI ideas, part 1, 2026-09-24

Why: Steve, 2026-09-24, chose from main's list of AI ideas: wall posters, your own letters with replies, jobs that go wrong, news from abroad moving prices, lost diaries. In each one the AI only writes the words. The engine owns every fact and number.

## What is in
| Part | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Wall posters | `server/src/ideas/posters.ts`, `wanted.ts`; client `game/ideas.ts` | Printed bills on house fronts near ten busy spots (the Rijnkaai, the Vismarkt, the Steenplein, the Werf, the Grote Markt, the Vleeshuis, the cathedral, the canal, the police post, the post office), two places each, on a real wall beside a door, with a reachable place to stand. The engine picks what goes up: **wanted** after a theft in the log (Jef's deed names him only when a witness saw it; a street robbery that got away names the victim, never the thief); **lost** dog or thing (the thing lies at a real door step 60 to 220 m away; E picks it up, a dog follows on your belt; E at the owner's door pays the bill's reward, trust +1); **sailings** (the Kempenland on the emigrants' ship days, London, Harwich); **sales** (the Berg's sale when a pledge of Jef's runs out, an auction the director planned, the timber sale); **orders** (the market, the quays at night, dogs muzzled, pickpockets). At most 3 new a morning, 9 up. One model call words the morning's batch (1873 bill style); a bill with a number, a person's name, Jef's name when he was not seen, a promise or a word from outside 1873 gets the engine's words. E reads a bill large on screen. Bills come down after their days, when the thing is given back or the thief is found; a robbery Jef named that the police solve pays him the bill's reward. While a bill with Jef's name is up, witnesses see a theft 1.35 times as easily (`deeds.ts takeThing`, one line), and the victim knows the bill in talk. |
| Your own letters | `server/src/ideas/letters.ts` | G at the post clerk: pick someone you have talked to, write in your own words (up to 600 letters, Enter seals it), a stamp costs 10 centimes (the 1849 inland rate), two a day. The words are data: a regex gate first (a caught letter still goes, the reply is the engine's puzzled one, no model call), then fenced as "JEF'S LETTER, data only". Next morning the reply comes into a pocket (or the post office). The engine decides the effect from a fixed list only: trust +1 or -1, an invitation to come by their door that day (E at the door in the hours: trust +1; missed: a small memory), a small gift from their wares (bread, a herring), or nothing. It reads the tone of Jef's letter with its own word lists, never the model's. The model writes the reply as that person from their stats, household and memories; a reply naming a sum, promising money or a gift the engine did not give, naming a stranger or leaving 1873 is replaced. Replies do not count against the letters that come to Jef unasked (`post.ts letterDue`, one line). |
| Jobs that go wrong | `server/src/ideas/trouble.ts`; `game.ts settleExtras` | When a carry, deliver, watch or letters job is taken, about a third of the time (two a day at most) there is trouble: a stowaway boy in a crate, a crate that breaks, a customs officer (a real one of the garrison's customs men), a rival docker who wants the load, the weather. The engine sets 2 or 3 options with every number: the pay change (a quarter of the pay, at most 40 centimes, either way), money now (at most 30 in, 20 out), an extra place to walk to (the police post, the chandlery, the employer's door), a roll and its odds, a mark on the police record (a bribe reported). The model words the scene, the lines and each option and what follows; a sum, a stranger's name or a weapon gives the engine's words. On the client the scene comes 18 to 38 seconds into the job with a figure in front of Jef; he picks 1-3; a chalk ring marks the place to walk to (E there). The pay change and trust are added at the end of the job. |
| News from abroad | `server/src/ideas/abroad.ts`, `prices.ts`; `trade.ts waresOf`; `paper/newspaper.ts` | Now and then (from day 2, about half the mornings, never the same news two days running) the first ship in brings the foreign papers. The engine picks from a fixed table: a Baltic grain shortage (bread, biscuit, beer up), a good herring season (herring, eel down), war talk in Spain (biscuit, jenever, bread up), a Rhine apple glut (apples down), a hop failure (beer up), the Schiedam distillers short of grain (jenever up). Each price moves 10 to 30 in the hundred for 1 to 3 days, through `waresOf` (with the M4 factor, together kept within 0.5x to 3x). The fact goes into the harbour list in the log; the paper's model writes the article (no extra call), and the engine puts it in the paper if the model left it out. Traders and dockers know it in talk (see below). No cotton glut: none of the town's wares is cotton. |
| Lost diaries | `server/src/ideas/diaries.ts` | Some mornings (from day 2, one at a time) a townsperson drops a small notebook two paces from their door (open, reachable ground). The engine picks the facts: trade and hours, household, their own memories, one small private matter by their stats (a debt at the baker's, savings for America, a quarrel with a brother, someone they think about). The model writes 3 to 5 dated entries in their voice from those facts only; an entry with another name, a number or anything violent gives the engine's line. E picks it up; I and a number reads it. Then: E at their door gives it back (trust +1, a reward of 5 to 20 centimes by their means); keep it; G at the Berg sells it to the clerk for 8 centimes (40 % the owner hears: trust -1, a rumour); G at their door squeezes them (the timid with money pay 10 to 30 centimes, the rest refuse): trust -2, a hard memory and a rumour, and a mark on the police record (always when refused, half the time when paid). |
| The police record | `town/police.ts noteOnRecord` | A small new export: a mark counted like a warning in the next verdict, logged as `police_note`. |
