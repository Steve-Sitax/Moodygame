## The rules and numbers (engine only)
Police points: velocipede 3, lantern 2, food 1; +1 when the owner saw it; half when given back; half (at least 1) when only rumour tells of it. Record: +1 per warning, +2 per fine, +3 per arrest, +1 per time he ran before; running from this agent +3 each time. His answer: owning up -1, giving it back -1 (if he still has it), a hungry man's excuse -1 (food only), denying it against the owner or two witnesses +1; his own words are read for these stances by a word list, nothing else (`stanceOf`).
Verdict: arrest at 8 points, or 5 when he ran from this agent, or 4 after two fines; else a fine at 3 points or with any warning or fine on the record; else a warning. Talk alone never jails a man who did not run and has fewer than two fines. A fine he cannot pay is arrest.
Fine: velocipede 60 c, lantern 25 c, food 10 c (halved when given back or only rumoured) + 10 c per earlier fine, a fifth off for owning up, rounded to 5, 10-150 c. Arrest takes the same fine, as far as his money goes.
Always: what he took goes back to its owner (pocket item removed, velocipede home); the agent remembers (6, arrest 8) and a rumour goes out ("Jef was warned / fined by the police for thieving", "Jef spent a night in the cell at the police post"); the agent's trust -1 on a fine or arrest; politie trust -1 (fine) or -2 (arrest).

Examples: a herring under the fishwife's nose, owned up: warning. Denied: fine 10 c. A velocipede in front of its owner: fine 60 c (50 c owned up). Ran from the agent over a lantern: arrest. Third time after two fines: arrest.

## Controls
- E near a velocipede: take it (or get back on your own); on it: W pedal, S brake (and push back when stopped), A/D steer (the view turns with it), or steer with the mouse (it turns toward where you look), Shift pedal hard, E get off.
- E near a standing lantern: take it. G at an open stall or shop table: take the food (E stays for talking to the keeper). L: hold the lantern up or put it away. F at Widow Peeters or the Werf chandler: buy a lantern.
- E near an owner who asks for it back: give it back.
- The police agent opens the talk himself: 1-3 answer, T your own words, E steps away (and walking off is running).

## Hooks for M4 (the NPC action layer and event director)
Server (`server/src/town/police.ts`, `deeds.ts`): `policeRespond(db, deedId)` (call the police about a deed; logged `police_called`), `policeDispatch(db, place)` (which agent on duty goes), `policeTick(db)` (every tick: visits due, rumour-driven visits), `policeArrived(db, agent)`, `policeFled(db)`, `policeOpen` / `policeAnswer` (the talk), `decide(...)` (the pure verdict), `policeState(db)` (record, visit, last verdict), `policeEvents(db, sinceId)` and `GET /api/police/events?since=` (the log rows above), `listDeeds(db)`, `deedRow(db, id)`, `takeThing`, `returnThing`.
Client (`client/src/game/deeds.ts`): `deeds.moveToward(puppet, x, z, pace)` is the only movement the police and owners use (M4 may replace it with its own walk on the crowd grid); `deeds.policeCome = (agent, name) => boolean` lets M4 take over the agent's walk (return true), then call `deeds.policeAtJef(agent, name)` when he stands before Jef or `deeds.policeRanFrom(agent)` when Jef gets away. This build has no "follow" of its own.
