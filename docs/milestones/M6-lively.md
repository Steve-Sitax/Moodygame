# M6 - The lively back streets and the cathedral quarter, 2026-09-24

Why (Steve, 2026-09-24): the quays are rich in detail, the back streets and the area round the cathedral feel thin. "10 ideas are a go." Everything that involves people lives through the town's residents and their days: real people with homes and trades, made by the engine.

## The engine
- `server/src/town/lively.ts`: 24 new residents made from the town's seed (their own random stream, their own ids `lv001...`, so the ids other parts add in place stay as they were), new trades in `places.ts`, work kind `round` (a route of stops and the way to the door at each). Schedules per trade (milk every morning and Sundays, mussels at dusk, the stalls on Sunday morning, the travellers inside noon to one). `ensureLively` adds them to an older save in place, once; `resetDb` gives a new week its own.
- `server/src/town/doorlife.ts` (pure, the client imports it): the week of door life per person from their day and stats (who scrubs, sits with her lace or knitting, leans out, takes flowers, and when), read by the clock and the weather; `gamesAt` for the children.
- `server/src/town/livelyWares.ts` and two small lines in `trade.ts`: milk 3 c (drunk at the cart), a roll 2 c and bread 6 c, boiled mussels 5 c, a birch broom 15 c, an old scarf 12 c, a clasp knife 35 c, a bag of coal 10 c, a votive candle 3 c, a holy picture 5 c, a rosary 20 c. Only while they are on their round (the trade rules' "shut" otherwise); the haggling floor and event factors apply as for every seller.
- `TOWN_SIZES.about` in `config.ts` +25 (the settings panel's promise).

## Not yet
- The cathedral model still has the two houses flanking the west front on the Handschoenmarkt, which were being pulled down between 1865 and 1875 (the landmark model, `build_landmarks.py`; the walk map also has them).
- The stalls stand at the edge of the church's ground (the map's apron between the lane and the church walls), with their roofs sloping back toward it, not against the house fronts themselves.
- Customers come out only for the sellers who cry and the grinder; the milk and the bread are carried to the door with nobody opening it.
- The coal man cries and waits; he does not carry sacks into cellars.
- The window figures, cats, hens and the goat are scenery: you cannot talk to them.
- No Antwerp source was found for the doorstep scrubbing, lace-makers outside, hens and goats in the courts, or the goods set out before the shops: plausible, marked as such.
