## Solid horses and carts

- Already solid before this work: the quay drays (bed and horse) and handcarts of `world/traffic.ts`, the goods train's horses and wagons, the omnibus body and team.
- Now solid too (`world/rijnkaai.ts` "movers": solid for Jef and for the drays, the train and the omnibus, not for the crowd's own paths): the carts townspeople push (the carters of the crowd, the households' carts on their trips) and the led dray of the transport errands. Parked household carts: the shafts are solid as well as the bed.
- The drays and handcarts of the quay traffic stop for Jef, for his cart, for people and for anything in the lane (as before); held up for 6 s by Jef or a thing, they move over to a clear side lane (1.7 m), go round and come back into their lane 10 m on. In a narrow street there is no side lane: they wait.
- Watchdog: a dray or handcart held up by a thing for 60 s with no way round backs off 6 m and tries again. An omnibus held up by a thing for 60 s backs off 9 m; standing on the rails in the train's way, after 8 s.
- Right of way: where an omnibus round lies on the rails' band (the zones are found from the tracks), the goods train goes first. The omnibus waits short of the stretch while the train is on it or coming (railway.ts `busy`), never on the rails.
- The deadlock at (64, 9): the Rijnkaai portal crane at its east end stood with a leg on the train's line where it curves off to the lock bridge, and the train waited for it for good (the omnibus only made it look face to face). The Rijnkaai cranes' working range now ends short of that crossing (54.3 instead of 66.8; `railway.ts`, one block next to the gatehouse clamp, the crane code otherwise untouched). Four berths remain.
- Routes checked against the solids (`__scheldemist.routeClips()`, `client/src/dev/routeClips.ts`): the Eilandje round clipped the farrier's forge at x 160: its east leg moved to x 158.6 (`traffic.ts`, and the copy in `possessions.ts` LANES). The water bailiff stood in the Werf lane (his post at the pontoon spot): posted 1.4 m further in (`game/town.ts`). It now lists nothing.
