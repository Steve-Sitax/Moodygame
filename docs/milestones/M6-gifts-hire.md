| The step executor | `server/src/director/steps.ts`; `client/src/game/steps.ts` | A routine is a list of typed steps for one person: walk to, follow, pick up, carry, buy, give, pay, talk to, wait, go in, sit, leave. | Gifts | `server/src/town/gifts.ts` | Jef's words must offer the gift ("give", "have a", "take this", "for you", ...), and the item must show in his words. The model's `item` only narrows the choice, so "a fish" means the herring he really has. The engine checks that the thing is his: from his pockets or the furniture piece in his arms, never a job's parcel, letters, a pawn ticket or money. A thing a person saw him steal is refused. If the owner saw it go, it goes back to them the M3h way (the deed is closed). An agent, customs man or soldier on duty takes nothing. Someone who dislikes Jef refuses, and a flattering or bribing gift annoys them. An honest person (honesty 7 or more) refuses a gift with strings attached. The well-off are too proud to take a cheap thing. Nobody takes more than three gifts a day. If the gift is taken, the item leaves Jef's inventory: the person keeps it, or eats it at once if they are truly hungry (a beggar, a street child) or a child. They remember it, and the town hears of it. |
| Worth and trust (anti-farming) | `gifts.ts giftTrust` | Worth to them = the price × their need ÷ (1 + 0.35 × wealth). Need: food counts 2.5 for a beggar, 1.8 for the poorest, down to 0.6 for the rich, and halves with each food gift that day. A lantern counts more for night workers, a rosary more for the pious. A worth under 3 gives no trust at all ("kind of you"). Every 6 of worth gives one point of credit, at most 2 per gift. That credit shrinks for each gift to the same person that day: all of the first gift counts, 40 % of the second, 15 % of the third, none after that. Whole points of credit become trust, never more than 2 points from gifts per person per game week, and never above trust 6 from gifts. The rest of the trust must come from deeds. While a gift is offered, the model's own trust change is held at 0. |
| Hand-over | `client/src/game/hands.ts` | Jef's forearm comes into view (sleeve, cuff, hand) holding the thing: a herring with a dark back, bread, an apple, a lantern or a parcel. His hand reaches out, lets go and pulls back. The person takes it into their hand (fish in paper, a parcel, a sack) and turns to talk. Their thanks is the talk's line. A small note says how it went ("He seems touched. It goes into his coat."). |
| A drink at the tavern | `server/src/town/treat.ts`; `client/src/game/hands.ts`, `interiors.ts` | The engine refuses: people under 16, the pious (piety 8 or more), people on duty or at work (a keeper at her stall), anyone in the small hours, the timid in the dark, anyone Jef is not trusted enough by (trust 1, or warmth 6, or gossip 7 needed), anyone already treated today, a tavern that is shut or more than 350 m away. The model also turns people down in character: a married woman would not go with a young man she had just met. If they accept, a routine runs: follow Jef (a step behind, lost at 40 m for 5 s), go in, sit, stay, leave. When Jef goes into any open tavern with them within 30 m of the door, they come in behind him and take a table with a free seat beside it. At the counter, G stands a round: two drinks at the keeper's own prices through the trade rules. Jef drinks his, so he gets tipsy too. At most 3 rounds. The first round gives trust through the gifts' ledger: at most 1 point, within the same weekly cap and ceiling. The guest gets merry (sways at 2 or more). Sitting at their table, F talks to them. |
| The loosened tongue | `treat.ts secretOf`, `treatContext` | After the first round the engine picks one thing the guest knows, in this order: a thief or runner in their own household; the town's recent dark doings (a theft, the police, a scuffle, a robbery); something they were part of; for a gossip, the pickpocket everyone in the taverns knows; the taverns' own gossip. It is never their errand for Jef, never Jef's own doings, never an event's bare start or end. The model says it in their words (it is in the talk prompt as a confidence to tell). A choice line "So what's the talk, then? Between us." gives the engine's words if Jef asks outright. It is told once. |
| Leaving | `treat.ts jefLeaves`, `treatTick` | When Jef goes out, the treat ends and the guest goes back to their day ("Thanks for the drink. I'll be off home."; merry: "Home, then. Which way is home?"). A tavern closing sends them home too. |
| Hired hands | `server/src/town/hire.ts` | Jef's words must name the work (carry, crates, cart, watch ...) and the wage. The wage must appear in his own words ("40 centimes", "half a franc", "two francs"); the model's number only chooses between them. With no sum, the person names their price. Their price: 5 c for coming, plus their day's pay ÷ 12 per load, by trade (a docker 10 c a load, a housewife 5 c). ×1.25 if they are at work; a keeper at her stall wants at least half a day's takings (80 + 30 × wealth); ×0.85 at trust 5; ×1.3 below trust 0; ×0.9 if warm; ×1.2 if greedy. They refuse: under 14 or over 62, on duty, the rich ("Do I look like a porter?"), trust −2 or lower, busy, the crew full, no carry job with goods left, goods they cannot reach on the walk grid (the pier), a wage under their price, a wage over 300 c ("what's the catch?"), all pay at the end from a stranger (trust under 2), and no coin for the first half. Pay: half up front and half at the end (the default), all now, or all at the end. The engine pays the rest when the work is done. If they stop early (Jef says stop, their day ends, they cannot get through), they are paid for the loads carried. If Jef cannot pay what he owes, the hand loses 2 trust and the town hears of it. |
| They really do it | `client/src/game/steps.ts` | Each load: walk to the goods, pick one up (on the shoulder, the carry clip), carry it to the goal and set it down on the job's own slots. It counts for Jef's job through `HaulRun.onPlaced`, under the board's own rules and pay. A crew works in parallel: each hand reserves the crate he walks to. The weak (under 18, over 55) leave a heavy load. Out of Jef's sight they carry on unseen at the hidden pace. After a reload, a hand who was carrying takes his load up again. If he cannot carry it on, he sets it down where he stands, so Jef can carry it. A hand whose working day ends goes home. |
| Jef's handcart | `hire.ts`, `server/src/town/handcart.ts` (`lendCart`, `returnCart`) | "Take my cart": an empty cart of Jef's standing within 40 m of the goods is lent. It leaves Jef's list while lent, and the hand is drawn pushing it (the crowd's own cart). He loads as many as fit per trip (5 crates, 3 barrels; `perTrip` by size and weight) and wheels them to the goal. If the cart is held up in a crowded market within 16 m of the goal, he carries the last few metres by hand. The cart goes back to Jef empty, where the hand left it (never on the chalk ring). If there is no way to the cart, he carries by hand. |
| Crew | `hire.ts crewCap` | 3 hands at first. One more if Jef has 200 c, one more at faction trust 3, one more at 5. At most 6. |
| Dishonest hands | `hire.ts dishonestPlan`, `walkedOff`, `answerAsk` | The engine rolls when the hand is hired. Chance to walk off with a load: 12 % for each point of honesty under 4; ×1.5 for a thief or a runner; ×1.5 if paid all up front; halved at trust 5. If he walks off, he goes about 45 m away with the load (with Jef's cart, the cart too). The job loses the goods. A `robbed` row goes on the record with what it cost Jef, so the M4 police case can take it up, and he gets no second half. Chance to stop halfway and ask for more (half the wage again): 35 % if greedy, 15 % if greedy and dishonest. He stands and asks. Jef answers in talk: "All right, N more" (engine money) or "No. A bargain is a bargain.". An honest hand grumbles and finishes; the others may down tools. If Jef does not answer within 120 game minutes, he quits. |

## Budget

The hired hands' lines take a new share, `HANDS_CALLS_PER_DAY = 2`, from what was left; never the reserve. The shares now come to 90 of 120. That leaves 30 for the board, the outcomes, the named people and the epilogue, the last 15 of them the reserve. The day's total stays 120 and the reserve 15.

## Not yet
- Deliver jobs (a parcel handed to a recipient) cannot be given to a hand; only carry jobs and watching.
- The model may refuse in words a gift the engine would allow. The engine only vetoes; it never forces a yes.
- A hand who walks off with Jef's cart: in the code, not tested in the browser. Asking for more and the watch: unit-tested only.
- Pushing a cart through a crowded market is slow. The crowd's carts stop for people, so near the goal the hand carries the last few metres by hand.
- The treat: the guest must be within 30 m of the door when Jef goes in. If Jef goes in far ahead, they wait at the door until he comes out.
- The mother's medal is not a gift ("Keep it").
- No model plan-making for routines: main builds that next, on `director/steps.ts`.
