## Research (quick, with sources)
- **Antwerp papers in 1873.** Het Handelsblad (van Antwerpen), from 1844; Le Precurseur (French, "political, commercial, maritime and literary", 1835-1902); De Koophandel (liberal, 1863-1897); Le Lloyd anversois (maritime, 1858); De Scheldegalm (1864). Belgian and French dailies of the time cost 5 to 15 centimes. We use the name Het Handelsblad for the period feel (Fientje already wraps fish in "yesterday's Handelsblad") at 5 centimes; the articles are ours. Sources: [Library of Congress, Het Handelsblad van Antwerpen](https://www.loc.gov/item/sn95058171/); [Le Matin d'Anvers, journaux cites](https://vanhassel.zone/matin/journaux.htm); [Gallica, Le Nouveau Journal at 5 centimes](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/btv1b9005183g.item).
- **The Berg van Barmhartigheid** (Mont-de-Piete), founded 6 February 1620 by Wenceslas Cobergher in the former English Nation warehouses. The sources put it in the **Venusstraat** (by the Hof van Liere), not the Lange Gasthuisstraat. Pledges were redeemed with about 12 in the hundred a year; loans ran up to a year; you got a pawn ticket, and a pledge not redeemed was sold at the Berg's sale. In 1878 it made about 170,000 loans for a town of about 200,000. Belgian monts lent about 2/3 of the value (more on gold and silver). Closed 1946, now the city archive. Sources: [Wikipedia NL](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berg_van_Barmhartigheid_(Antwerpen)); [TOPA, a walk to the Berg](https://topa.be/op-reis-in-eigen-stad/bergwandeling/barmhartigheid/); [Open Monumentendag 2021](https://magazine.antwerpen.be/open-monumentendag-2021/berg-van-barmhartigheid).
- **Post and telegraph.** The Hotel des Postes on the Groenplaats dates from 1878 (in 1873 the office stood elsewhere; the source does not say where). Inland letters: 10 centimes under 30 km (1849 tariff). Telegrams: 50 centimes for 20 words (the 1865 form, used until 1876). Sources: [Inventaris Onroerend Erfgoed, Hoofdkantoor van de Posterijen](https://inventaris.onroerenderfgoed.be/erfgoedobjecten/4018); [Linn's, Belgium's first stamps](https://www.linns.com/news/us-stamps-postal-history/2016/january/identifying-belgiums-first-stamp-issue-the-epaulettes-of-1849.html); [Belgian telegraph stamps](http://telstamps.org.uk/Telegraphs/World/Belge.html).

## What is in
| Part | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The press in the town | `server/src/paper/town.ts` | Runs on every start and after a new game (`db.ts`). Three **newsboys**: boys of 10 to 14 from three different households (the nearest home to each corner) become trade `newsboy`: at their corner 6:15 to 11:30 (Rijnkaai by the hiring board, the Vismarkt corner, the Grote Markt), at play in the afternoon, every day. The **post and telegraph office**: a free house (nobody lives or works there) near (-210, 112), between the Grote Markt and the Vleeshuis; its clerk is a NEW resident (`postclerk`, trade `post_clerk`, lives above the office, 8:00-12:30 and 13:30-19:00, church on Sunday). The **Berg**: the existing pawnshop by the Vleeshuis; its keeper is now labelled "clerk of the Berg van Barmhartigheid". Jef's mother's silver medal is sewn into his coat (no pocket slot). Record: world_state `press`. |
| The morning | `server/src/paper/routes.ts` | After each new board (a hook in `makeBoard`, `boardExtras`) and on the tick: the Berg's sale (pledges past their day), the post office's round, sometimes (55 %) an event announced for later today (planned through the director's `planEvent`, 150-180 minutes ahead, a template that fits), then the paper, then maybe a letter. |
| The paper | `server/src/paper/newspaper.ts` | The engine writes the harbour master's list into the log each morning (2-3 ships in, 1-2 out; the first two carry what the board's carry work unloads, never yesterday's ships), the weather and the prices. The facts: yesterday's log (events that started, thefts, police, what someone SAW, as rumours with a witness), this morning's lines, planned events, the best-paid work on the board. Thieves are named by initials only; Jef's unseen deeds never reach the paper. The model (hook `newspaper`) writes 4-6 articles (each names its fact by number), the shipping lines and the newsboys' cry. The engine drops an article about an unknown fact or a repeat, replaces one whose numbers are not in its fact, and a ship line that does not name its ship. Fallback: the engine's paper from the same facts, heaviest first. The headline goes into the log (the director reads it in `eventSlice`: the small hook, no director file changed) and into world_fact (the talk of the town). The page adds a WANTED column of the day's work in the engine's words. |
| Buying and reading | `server/src/trade.ts`, `client/src/game/pockets.ts`, `client/src/game/press.ts` | The newsboys sell through the trade rules (`TRADE_WARES.newsboy`, 5 c; F or B at the boy). One of today's; yesterday's is left on a bench. `item.ref` (new column) points a pocket row at its paper, letter or pawn ticket. 1-6 in the pockets reads it: the paper on yellowed newsprint with a blackletter masthead, three columns and SHIPPING INTELLIGENCE; D throws a paper or letter away. |
| Newsboys' cries | `client/src/game/press.ts` | Within 28 m of a boy at work, every 8-14 s: the cry (or "Het Handelsblad, 5 centimes! ...") as an M4 bubble with the made voice (`bubbles.show`), and a gesture. |
| The post office's round | `server/src/paper/post.ts`, job task kind `letters` | Once a day, not on Sunday: 3 to 5 doors of different households in one part of the town, walked nearest-first; the door steps are the homes every path check covers. Pay within the board's tier band (30 + 12 a door + distance/15). On the board and at the counter (F by the clerk). Fetch the letters at the counter (E; the office must be open), E at each door. The server checks the place and marks each stop; at the end it pays by its own count, never the client's. |
| Letters to Jef | `server/src/paper/post.ts` | Not on day 1; at most one a day and four a week; at least one by day 3. The engine picks the writer from the memories: someone Jef wronged (a complaint, or an unpaid errand "to make it right"), someone he helped (thanks, or a paid errand), someone he talked to (an errand, a telegram, or news from the log), or a stranger with a strange request (a sealed letter to a door, or a telegram). The engine sets the errand and its pay (letter 20-40 c, stranger 40-70 c, telegram 50 c fee back plus 20-60 c) and builds it as a job (`letters`, from the sender's door or the post counter). The model (hook `letter`) writes the words and the telegram (at most 20 words). A letter that names another sum, words like "five francs", or an enclosure or gift gives way to the engine's letter. The letter goes in a pocket in the morning ("A boy brings you a letter"), or waits at the post office when the pockets are full. T in the letter takes the errand. |
| The telegraph | `post.ts sendTelegram` | A counter action on an errand: E at the counter pays the 50 c fee (the job pays it back), the clerk sends the words. |
| The Berg | `server/src/paper/pawn.ts` | F by the clerk opens the counter. It lends on durable goods only (the medal, worth 90; a lantern, 40): 4/5 on silver, 2/3 on the rest, down to 5 c (medal 70 c, lantern 25 c). Interest 5 in the hundred a day begun, at least 1 c (the game week stands in for the Berg's year). Redeem by the end of the third day after (and by Sunday); else the pledge is sold at the Berg's sale that night and the ticket goes. The ticket takes the pledge's pocket slot. Food: "the Berg lends on goods that keep". |
| Clerks' remarks | `post.ts clerkRemark` | After a pawn, a redemption or a telegram: the model (hook `clerk`) writes one line from the engine's situation; a number not in the situation, a sum in words or a gift gives way to the fallback. Shown as a bubble over the clerk. Changes nothing. |
| Signs | `client/src/game/press.ts` | "POST OFFICE / TELEGRAPH" and "BERG VAN BARMHARTIGHEID" boards over the two doors (canvas texture, psx material). |
