# M5 - Needs and week, 2026-09-23

Why: Steve picked M5. The game gets a clock, a week, a bed, rent, and an end.

## What is in
| Part | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Clock | `server/src/day.ts`, client `game/day.ts` | While you play, the client asks for a tick every 5 s. The server moves the clock 15 game minutes per tick, at most one tick per 4 s. 6:00 to midnight is about 6 real minutes. Time stops when you pause (Esc) or a night sheet is up. Top left: weekday and time, and "rent due" until you pay. |
| Needs fall | `applyHour()` | Food -1 every 4 h. Sleep -1 every 2 h. Warmth -1 every 3 h by day, every 2 h at night (20:00 to 7:00). Health -1 an hour while any need is 0; +1 every 6 h while all are 5 or more. Needs drawing now has four rows: loaves, flames, moons (sleep), hearts (health). |
| Light and weather | `world.setTimeOfDay()`, `world.setWeather()` | Fog colour, sky light and fog distance follow the hour. Gas lamps are out by day and lit at dusk. Each morning the engine rolls the weather: fog 40 %, mist 35 %, clear 25 %. Monday is always fog. Steve, 2026-09-23: "we do not always need fog". Claude's board and the people's talk know the weather. |
| Doss house | `rijnkaai.ts` `dossHouse()`, `DOSS_POS` | The Sint-Andries alley: a gate in the fence between the Katoennatie and the Hessenatie, a lane, and a door under a lantern. E: go to bed (from 18:00, or earlier when sleep is 2 or less). Before 18:00 the landlady sends you off. F: pay the week's rent, 150 c. The path check includes the gate. |
| The night | `sleep()` | Bed: sleep 10, warmth +3, food -2, health +1 if you ate. Still out at midnight: you sleep rough (sleep 6, warmth -3, health -2). Sunday with no rent: no bed, you sleep rough. A job still in hand at night fails and its parcel leaves your pocket. The night sheet shows the day's summary; E wakes you at 6:00 at the alley gate. |
| New board each morning | `index.ts` `afterNight()` | Claude writes a new board for the new day. The old "refill when empty" stand-in and the "+1 hour per job" stand-in are gone. The board says "No more work today" when nothing is open. |
| Memory fades | `consolidate()` | Each night every memory and fact loses 1 weight (floor 1). Weight-1 memories older than 3 days are deleted. |
| The end | `endGame()`, `hooks/epilogue.ts` | After Sunday night, or when health reaches 0. Claude writes the epilogue from the log, money, needs, trust and what each person thinks of Jef: a title and two paragraphs. 20 s timeout and a fixed fallback text. N starts a new week (`POST /api/new-game` wipes the save). |
| Plain English | `server/src/text.ts` | Steve, 2026-09-23: too many Dutch words. Dutch only in names of people, places, firms and ships (and jenever). All prompts carry `LANGUAGE_RULE`; `plainEnglish()` strips Dutch forms of address from model text. Fixed lines rewritten. docs/08 #8 and docs/03 updated. |
| Dev | `POST /api/dev/set` | Dev only: set day, hour, minute, needs or money, to test the night without waiting. |
