## The AI budget now (server/src/config.ts)
120 calls a day, 15 kept in reserve. Shares: townspeople 40, director 12, conversations 10,
families 3, surprises 3, tavern 6, Poesje 2, paper 6, homes 2, ideas 4, confession 2, hiring 1,
ballads 2, sermon 1, hands 2, routines 6 (102 in all). The job board, outcomes, the named quay four
and the epilogue use what is left before the reserve. Each share stops at its line; when it is gone
the engine writes the words itself.

## Testing (how the helpers and main did it)
- Never on Steve's save: a `.backup()` copy of `data/game.sqlite`, a server with `SCHELDEMIST_DB` and
  `SCHELDEMIST_PORT`, a vite with its own config (copy one of `data/vite-test-*.config.mjs`).
- The preview tabs often cannot reach local ports; a headless Chrome over its debug port (CDP, with
  Node's own WebSocket) works, at 1280x800 or 1920x1080. `Emulation.setFocusEmulationEnabled` for
  focus-dependent checks.
- A game hour is 20 real seconds: long runs starve a test Jef; restore needs on the copy only.
- `__scheldemist`: `paths()` must be `[]`, `perf(n)`, `step(s)`, `shotFrom(name, from, to, fogFar)`.
