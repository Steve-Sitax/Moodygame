# M6 - Street ballads, the Sunday sermon, the Vleeshuis wine cellar, 2026-09-24

Why: Steve chose these AI ideas on 2026-09-24: a ballad singer who sings each day's new ballad about what really happened in town, a Sunday sermon about the week's sins and scandals, and a full, lit wine cellar in the Vleeshuis (it was Peyrot's wine warehouse in 1873; `m6l_vh_cellar.jpg` looked empty and too dark).

The engine picks every fact and owns every number. The model only words the ballad and the sermon (schema, 20 s, then the engine's words).
