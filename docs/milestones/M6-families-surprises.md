# M6 - Families who share and act; the AI surprise pack, 2026-09-24

Why (Steve, 2026-09-24): "Families that live together and share experiences. I.e. if I am rude to a wife she will tell her husband when he is near and he might come talk to me afterwards or call cops on me. Or come beat me up or mug me. AI decides upon stats of the people what will happen but make choices that are more in favor of interactions with the player." And the unpredictability pack from `M6-plan.md`: a fortune teller, strangers off the ships, private schemes, rumours that twist, dreams.

Rules held: the engine owns every number and every outcome; the model only words things and proposes from fixed lists (schema, 20 s, a fallback every time). No combat (docs/08 #10): danger is narrated.

## Families who share and act
| Part | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| News | `server/src/director/families.ts` `scanNews` | A townsperson's own notable meeting with Jef (a memory with a gist and a tone of -2..-1 or +1..+2: rude, kind, a theft, a gift, a lie found out) becomes a `family_news` row for each household member of 13 or more. The talk prompt now asks the model always to write a rumour when Jef is rude, insults, lies, helps or gives. |
| When it is told | `together`, `familyTick` | The ENGINE decides: when both are at home by their schedules, or both out within 12 m of each other (the client's positions, else the schedule). Waits up to a game day. The street's gossip no longer carries someone's first-hand news into their own household (`rumours.ts`); from other people it still may. |
| What the listener gets | `shareNews` | A second-hand memory with its source: "My wife Marie told me: Jef called ...", heard from the teller. If Jef is within 45 m and they are in the street, the teller first walks over (an M4 `talk_to`, purpose "share") and it plays as bubbles. |
| The reaction | `allowedReactions`, `decideReaction` | One model call (hook `family_share`) writes the 2-4 lines AND proposes the listener's reaction from a fixed list: none, talk_calm, talk_angry, demand_apology, demand_payment, warn_others, call_police, knock_down, mug; for good news thank, invite_supper, gift, warn_others. The prompt lists only what the listener's stats allow, heaviest first, and says to prefer what brings them to Jef. The engine's weights count "comes to Jef" reactions 2.5 times (Steve's lean). A refused or unknown proposal gets the engine's pick and the engine's lines (nobody says yes while the engine said no). Sums are the engine's (a demand at most 30 c). |
| Who may do what | `allowedReactions` | Adults only; the police never. Calm talk: warmth 4+ or temper 5-. Angry talk: temper 5+. Payment: only when the fact is a loss. Police: 18+, honest or timid, a bad enough fact, an agent on duty. A menace: a man of 18-60, temper 7+, courage 5+, grudge 5+ (tone, temper, trust, what else he holds against Jef), a rough trade (docker, natie man, porter, carter, boatman, sailor, pickpocket); a mugging also needs honesty 4- and greed 5+ (or a pickpocket). Visits by day (7-21), a menace to 23:00. |
| A visit | `startReaction`, `startSeek`, client `actions.ts seek` | The listener sets out (a new action kind "seek") when free and Jef is within 350 m, at most two at once. The client walks them up to Jef; on arrival the talk window opens with them. Their opening is the model's line when it was written for that reaction, else the engine's ("My wife tells me you ..."). Two engine lines lead: sorry or defiant (trust +1 / -1; a hot head who is defied may come back rougher later), pay the engine's sum or refuse (then an honest one goes to the police), thanks, supper (food +3, warmth +2, once a day, the screen dims with a line), a gift from their own wares into the pockets. The rest of the talk is the ordinary talk. |
| Warn others | `warnOthers` | Up to three of the listener's circle hear it at once ("X warned me about Jef: ..."), or the good word. |
| The police | `actions.ts` fetch_police chain | The listener walks to the agent on duty and reports (the M4 conversation, now worded as a complaint); the agent then comes to find Jef for a word. Nobody is arrested or fined for words. |
| A menace | `startMenace`, `resolveMenace`, `talkDown`; client `families.ts` | He walks up and has his say in a bubble. A panel: walk away fast (12 m: "ran"), T talk him down in your own words, P pay the engine's demand (a mugger 40 % of Jef's purse, 5-40 c; a knock-down 10 c). 12 real seconds, paused while typing. An agent within 30 m or 6 people within 15 m stop it (on arrival and at the end). Talk-down: the gate and the fence of M3; the model (hook `resident_talkdown`, the townspeople's share) only reads the stance (apologetic, calm, pleading, defiant, insulting, nonsense) and offers two lines; the ENGINE rolls with his warmth, temper and grudge. Otherwise the screen dims and a line tells it: knock-down health -2, mugging health -1 and the demand taken; never below 1 health; nobody dies. A mugging is a logged `robbed` (the M4 police case and the director's follow-up work on it), a knock-down a logged `assaulted`; world_event `mugged` / `knocked_down`, his memory, up to five witnesses' memories, the town's rumour "Jef was knocked down ... by Theofiel Cuypers". One menace a day in town. |

## The surprise pack
| Part | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Madame Zelie | `server/src/town/visitors.ts`, `surprises.ts`; client table in `game/families.ts` | A new resident, added in place to any save (db.ts): an old fortune teller at a folding table with a red cloth and five cards on the east side of the Grote Markt (-240, 101), 9:30-17:30 with a dinner hour; a room nearby. "Read my fortune (5 centimes)": the engine takes the coin, the model (hook `resident_fortune`, townspeople's share) reads the cards from Jef's recent days and picks the promise (meeting, loss, gift, stranger); weapons or sums in its words: the engine's cards. Once a day. She is kept from events all her working day. |
| The promise | `keepPromise`, `promiseThread`, `director.ts` | Stored hidden (`world_state fortune_promise`), due within 2 days. The director's prompt gets "A PROMISE TO KEEP"; the engine keeps it itself (in the director's fallback path and hourly): a warm person comes to meet Jef, someone with food gives him some, a coin of at most 5 c slips through a hole in his pocket, or a stranger arrives and comes to find him. Lapses after its day. |
| Strangers | `visitors.ts`, `surprises.ts` | Five places kept as residents (a sailor, a merchant, a runaway girl, a preacher, a card player), away (all day "home", out of sight, reserved from everything) until one arrives: by day, about one in five hours, never two at once, not the same kind twice. The ENGINE picks the kind and the stay (1-3 days); the model (hook `stranger_arrive`) gives the name, origin, story, goal, secret and greeting; a bad or taken name, or words with a weapon or a sum: the engine's stranger. Their day walks the town (quays, squares, a tavern); the client patches the name and the day into the town it loaded. In talk: their own greeting, their story in the prompt, the secret never said outright. "Can I help you with that?": an engine-built errand (find a boatman, a chandler, a laundress, the sexton or a publican, ask the question, bring the answer back, 15 c from the engine). Then they leave. |
| Schemes | `planSchemes`, `schemeTick`, `runScheme` | Each morning 2-3, the engine's: a lost dog, a debt paid, a courtship, a grudge settled, between neighbours. At its hour A walks to B (M4 `talk_to`, purpose "scheme") and they talk in bubbles (the model's lines when Jef is within 45 m and the conversations' share allows, else the engine's). The outcome is the engine's roll. Talking to A that day: "You look troubled. Can I help?" raises the odds; if it goes well, A trusts Jef more and the town hears "Jef helped ...". |
| Rumours that twist | `town/rumours.ts` `driftWording`, `withinFact`; `surprises.ts twistRumours` | The fact stays in `npc_memory.gist`; the wording people say is the new `told_as`. At each telling the engine may drift it a little (a same-meaning word or a hedge); once a day after noon the model rewords up to six fresh tellings (hook `rumour_twist`). Every telling must pass `withinFact`: starts with "Jef", no theft, blow, police, drink or lie the fact lacks, no weapon, no new name, number or sum, no "not" turned round. The original fact is kept, and each model drift is a `rumour_twist` row with both. |
| Dreams | `dreamOf`, `familyRoutes.ts` | When a night passes (any route that moves the day: the bed, a home, the cell, the tick past midnight), a short dream from the day's log (hook `dream`); with a weapon, blood or a number, the engine's dream. It shows on the night sheet ("You dream. ..."), or as a line if the sheet is gone. No number moves. |

## Budget (`server/src/config.ts`)
The day stays 80, the reserve 15. Two new shares, taken from existing ones:
- `DIRECTOR_CALLS_PER_DAY` 12 -> 9 and `CONVO_CALLS_PER_DAY` 10 -> 7;
- `FAMILY_CALLS_PER_DAY` 3 (hook `family_share`: the lines and the proposal in one call);
- `SURPRISE_CALLS_PER_DAY` 3 (`stranger_arrive`, `rumour_twist`, `dream`).
The fortune and the talk-down ride in the townspeople's 30 (`resident_*`); the schemes' lines in the conversations' share. When a share is gone the engine words it.
