# M4 - Events, the director and people who act, 2026-09-24

Why: Steve, 2026-09-23: "Ai should be able to change his path, i.e. follow me, but if it takes too long stop it. If I ask him to talk to someone I should be able to see their conversation as bubbles and some murmuring sound that sounds like speech. Ai should be able to drive actions. A tick rate that fires an AI session to see if things need to happen, or random events, or completely surprising events, i.e. a wedding at the church. No overlapping events. Keep track of everything happening on the server in a database so AI can use it." Design: `data/m4-draft/NOTES.md` (kept as the design record).

Branch `m4`, worktree `D:\Code\MoodyGame-m4`, run with `npm run dev:alt` (game 5183, server 8797).

## What is in
| Part | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Actions | `server/src/director/vocab.ts`, `actions.ts`; `client/src/game/actions.ts` | The talk reply carries an optional `action` (follow, go_to, wait, talk_to, look_for, fetch_police, give, stop; a target, minutes, item, amount, reason). The ENGINE checks it in order: known kind, one thing per person, at most 3 asked in talk, not reserved (an event, the M3h police visit, an employer's post), the trade (a keeper at the stall stays, a child questions nobody), the stats (follow needs trust 2, warmth 6, or the police with a robbery; the timid stay put at night), the target (a known person or place, not indoors, walkable), the distance (go_to 400 m, talk_to 150 m). On a refusal the person's line is REPLACED by the engine's ("I don't know you well enough for that."); on acceptance the limit is added ("I can spare you half an hour, no more."). Every action is a row in `npc_action` (survives a reload); the client walks the person (follow a step behind, lost at 40 m for 5 s, stopped at the water's edge; to a place; waiting; looking about within 40 m; up to someone to talk; to the nearest agent on duty) and reports arrived, lost, blocked, done. The server ends what runs out of time with a line. Time limits are game minutes and large (a game hour is 20 real seconds): the police follow up to 720, others 480, children 240, never under 240; the model's own minutes are story minutes taken twelve-fold. |
| Give | `actions.ts` | Never money, with two engine exceptions: an item from their wares becomes an offer ("5 centimes, and you pay at the counter"), and the thief who robbed Jef (a logged `robbed` not yet returned) hands the logged amount back once, when asked straight. Everything else: "My money stays in my pocket." |
| The police case | `actions.ts`, `convo.ts` | "I was robbed, follow me" to an agent: the follow is allowed on the robbery alone. "That's him, Hubert": the agent goes to Hubert, and the questioning is a conversation with the outcome FIXED by the engine from the log: guilty (the logged amount back to Jef, memories on both, the rumour "Jef got his money back through the police") or innocent (a denial, the accused thinks less of Jef). `fetch_police` is a chain: the neighbour walks to the agent on duty, reports (a conversation), the agent goes to where Jef was, then looks for the thief the log names (or waits for Jef). The engine's own follow-up (`director.ts followUp`): an unsolved robbery sends the nearest agent once, in daylight. |
| Conversations | `server/src/director/convo.ts`; `client/src/game/bubbles.ts`; `soundscape.ts speech()` | Two townspeople talk in the street (hook `npc_convo`, its own share of 10 calls a day, never the reserve): 2-6 lines by the model with both persons' stats, moods and what they know of Jef, the purpose (chat, question, invite, argue, report) and the fixed outcome in the prompt. The engine applies: memories, trust to Jef by at most one each, a rumour passed on is the engine's own gist (never the model's words), the purse only for the fixed verdict. Engine lines per purpose when the model is late. Shown as paper tags over their heads (1.75 m, children 1.3 m), one line at a time taking turns, 2.5-4 s by length, at most six, hidden beyond 30 m or behind the camera, with a voice made in code: noise and a glottal sawtooth through two vowel formants at 4-6 syllables a second (men 110 Hz, women 210, children 280, the old a tenth lower), through the same fog and air as every placed sound. No new recordings. |
| The director | `server/src/director/director.ts` | Thinks once a game hour at most (hook `director_think`, 12 calls a day, never the reserve), sooner after a notable fact (a robbery, an arrest, a job's end, a heavy event, nightfall), with a 30-minute gap, async with a lock; the answer is checked against the state when it arrives. It sees the day, the hour, the weather, the open threads (an unsolved robbery, what runs), the events held today, the event log slice, the place ids, the templates and the primitives. Decision: nothing, an event (a template id, or "custom" made of the primitives), or a follow-up. Every number goes through the scheduler's clamps; a bad place or an overlap is refused and logged. Late, wrong or over budget: the engine picks (an open thread first, else 35 % an hour from 8:00 to 21:00 of a template that fits the day and the hour). |
| The scheduler | `server/src/director/scheduler.ts`, `templates.ts`; `client/src/game/events.ts`; `soundscape.ts eventSound()` | `town_event` rows made of stages the engine plays by game time: gather (a role, 2-24 fitting free residents, attend actions to a ring round the place, so nobody is in two things), procession (a column to a place, the followers behind the one ahead), sound (bells, music, murmur, handbell) and mood, props (crates, barrels, sacks, flowers, black cloth), talk (a conversation between two of the gathered), notice and rumour (world_fact, in the talk prompts, memories for the gathered), price (0.5x-3x on an item until the end, through `trade.waresOf`), close/open (a shop, stall place or tavern, through `trade.atWork` and the stalls' tarpaulins), job (a carry job on the board through the board's own rules and pay), weather (once a day). Refused when it would share a place or stand within 60 m of another event in its window (20 minutes margin), when two already run, or after four in a day. The end releases the people, takes the props and the sounds away, reopens the places and puts the prices back. Templates: wedding (west door of the cathedral, bells, a talk, a procession to Den Engel, music), funeral (an old resident's door, black cloth, a handbell procession to the cathedral), street musicians, the emigrant ship (a Red Star notice, a carry job), the fish auction (herring at 0.8x), a quarrel at the market (two sellers argue in bubbles, a crowd, the police). |
| The event log | `server/src/director/eventlog.ts`, `schema.ts` | `world_event` (day, hour, minute, kind, verb, actor, target, place, x, z, engine text, outcome, ref, weight) and `world_event_who`. Two SQLite triggers mirror what older code already writes: every `log` row (kind and weight by verb) and every rumour someone saw start (`npc_memory` with a gist, source seen). Actions, events, conversations and the director write directly. `eventSlice` gives the prompts the newest and the weightiest lines within a budget (the heavy ones survive the trim). A player's typed words never land here. Additive migration: old saves keep working. |
| Talk hooks | `server/src/town/talk.ts` | `talkHooks` (installed by the director's routes): the action rules in the system prompt; the person's task now, the people near (from the client's sync), the talk of the town (notices, rumours), the robbery on the quays, and a short slice of the events they were in, in the prompt; the proposal check on the way out. The stub lines of the older tests carry no action and still pass. |
| Budget | `server/src/config.ts` | 80 a day: townspeople 30 (actions ride in the same reply), director 12, conversations 10, the rest 28 for the board, outcomes, the named people and the epilogue. The director and the conversations never take the last 15. |
| Routes | `server/src/director/routes.ts` | `GET /api/actions` (actions, recent conversations, live events, closed places), `POST /api/actions/sync` (Jef's place and who is in the street, every 2 s while actions run), `POST /api/actions/:id/report`, `GET /api/events`, dev `POST /api/dev/director {think|template}`; the ticks in `/api/tick`; a new game clears everything; "Jef stopped X for a talk" logged once per meeting. Pushes: `actions` (with the ending line), `events`, `convo`. |
| Dev | `main.ts`, the Dev menu | Buttons "Director: think now" and "Event: wedding / funeral / musicians / emigrant ship / fish auction / quarrel"; `__scheldemist.actions.info()`, `.events.info()`, `.bubbles.info()` and `.shown`. |
