# M4 - Events, the director and people who act, 2026-09-24

Why: Steve, 2026-09-23: "Ai should be able to change his path, i.e. follow me, but if it takes too long stop it. If I ask him to talk to someone I should be able to see their conversation as bubbles and some murmuring sound that sounds like speech. Ai should be able to drive actions. A tick rate that fires an AI session to see if things need to happen, or random events, or completely surprising events, i.e. a wedding at the church. No overlapping events. Keep track of everything happening on the server in a database so AI can use it." Design: `data/m4-draft/NOTES.md` (kept as the design record).

| Actions | `server/src/director/vocab.ts`, `actions.ts`; `client/src/game/actions.ts` | The talk reply carries an optional `action` (follow, go_to, wait, talk_to, look_for, fetch_police, give, stop; a target, minutes, item, amount, reason). The ENGINE checks it in order: known kind, one thing per person, at most 3 asked in talk, not reserved (an event, the M3h police visit, an employer's post), the trade (a keeper at the stall stays, a child questions nobody), the stats (follow needs trust 2, warmth 6, or the police with a robbery; the timid stay put at night), the target (a known person or place, not indoors, walkable), the distance (go_to 400 m, talk_to 150 m). On a refusal the person's line is REPLACED by the engine's ("I don't know you well enough for that."); on acceptance the limit is added ("I can spare you half an hour, no more."). Every action is a row in `npc_action` (survives a reload); the client walks the person (follow a step behind, lost at 40 m for 5 s, stopped at the water's edge; to a place; waiting; looking about within 40 m; up to someone to talk; to the nearest agent on duty) and reports arrived, lost, blocked, done. The server ends what runs out of time with a line. Time limits are game minutes and large (a game hour is 20 real seconds): the police follow up to 720, others 480, children 240, never under 240; the model's own minutes are story minutes taken twelve-fold. |
| Give | `actions.ts` | Never money, with two engine exceptions: an item from their wares becomes an offer ("5 centimes, and you pay at the counter"), and the thief who robbed Jef (a logged `robbed` not yet returned) hands the logged amount back once, when asked straight. | The police case | `actions.ts`, `convo.ts` | "I was robbed, follow me" to an agent: the follow is allowed on the robbery alone. | Conversations | `server/src/director/convo.ts`; `client/src/game/bubbles.ts`; `soundscape.ts speech()` | Two townspeople talk in the street (hook `npc_convo`, its own share of 10 calls a day, never the reserve): 2-6 lines by the model with both persons' stats, moods and what they know of Jef, the purpose (chat, question, invite, argue, report) and the fixed outcome in the prompt. | The director | `server/src/director/director.ts` | Thinks once a game hour at most (hook `director_think`, 12 calls a day, never the reserve), sooner after a notable fact (a robbery, an arrest, a job's end, a heavy event, nightfall), with a 30-minute gap, async with a lock; the answer is checked against the state when it arrives. It sees the day, the hour, the weather, the open threads (an unsolved robbery, what runs), the events held today, the event log slice, the place ids, the templates and the primitives. Decision: nothing, an event (a template id, or "custom" made of the primitives), or a follow-up. Every number goes through the scheduler's clamps; a bad place or an overlap is refused and logged. Late, wrong or over budget: the engine picks (an open thread first, else 35 % an hour from 8:00 to 21:00 of a template that fits the day and the hour). |
| The scheduler | `server/src/director/scheduler.ts`, `templates.ts`; `client/src/game/events.ts`; `soundscape.ts eventSound()` | `town_event` rows made of stages the engine plays by game time: gather (a role, 2-24 fitting free residents, attend actions to a ring round the place, so nobody is in two things), procession (a column to a place, the followers behind the one ahead), sound (bells, music, murmur, handbell) and mood, props (crates, barrels, sacks, flowers, black cloth), talk (a conversation between two of the gathered), notice and rumour (world_fact, in the talk prompts, memories for the gathered), price (0.5x-3x on an item until the end, through `trade.waresOf`), close/open (a shop, stall place or tavern, through `trade.atWork` and the stalls' tarpaulins), job (a carry job on the board through the board's own rules and pay), weather (once a day). Templates: wedding (west door of the cathedral, bells, a talk, a procession to Den Engel, music), funeral (an old resident's door, black cloth, a handbell procession to the cathedral), street musicians, the emigrant ship (a Red Star notice, a carry job), the fish auction (herring at 0.8x), a quarrel at the market (two sellers argue in bubbles, a crowd, the police). |
| The event log | `server/src/director/eventlog.ts`, `schema.ts` | `world_event` (day, hour, minute, kind, verb, actor, target, place, x, z, engine text, outcome, ref, weight) and `world_event_who`.
