# M3 - Talk and remember, 2026-09-23

Goal (docs/06): dialogue with Sooi and Fientje, memory in SQLite, Fientje repeats what Sooi saw.
Added on Steve's call: people hand over goods, owners react when you take their goods, lift and stack any goods, jump and crouch. No combat in the demo (docs/08 #10).

## How to run
`npm run dev` in the repo root, then http://localhost:5173. Delete `data/game.sqlite` to start over.
Keys: WASD, mouse, Shift hurry, **Space jump**, **C or Ctrl crouch**, **E** use / talk / lift / set down, **F** job action, **1-3** answer, **T** type your own line.

## What is in
| Part | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| People on the quay | `client/src/game/people.ts` | Sooi at the hiring board, Widow Peeters at her loading door, Tuur on the pier with his rowing boat, Fientje at her fish cart, a sailor on the Anna Maria. Grey-box bodies with their own look. They turn to face you. |
| Personas | `server/src/npcs.ts` | Claude writes each persona once from a short seed (traits, loves, hates, wants, fears, secret, speech, look). Hand-written fallback. |
| Relationship | `npc_relationship` | Trust, times met, last seen, "how I see Jef". Trust moves at most +-2 per conversation (engine clamp). Hidden from the client: the API sends words, never numbers (docs/08). |
| Memory | `npc_memory` | One sentence per memory, seen or heard, weight 1-10. The top 8 go into that NPC's prompt, nobody else's. Job outcomes, talks and owner events all write memories. |
| Gossip | `spreadGossip()` | A seen memory of weight 6+ reaches Fientje as "heard", two points lighter, once. She tells it her way. Spread to others comes with the M5 sleep step. |
| dialogue / free_reply hooks | `server/src/hooks/dialogue.ts` | Prompt: stable rules first, then persona, relationship, memories, the kaai lately, the job facts, the meeting so far, the scene. Output: line, mood, 3 choices, trust change, memory note, view of Jef, end. |
| Opening prefetch | `POST /api/npc/:id/near` | When you come within 10 m, the opening line is written in the background. In the test it was ready in 0.5 s. |
| Free-text walls | docs/03 | 1: no tools. 2: typed text fenced as "JEF SAYS". 3: schema only. 4: gate before any call (300 chars, control chars stripped, one line per 5 s, regex list, canned in-character reply, logged). 5: no machine data in prompts (tested). |
| Owners | `client/src/game/jobs.ts` | Goods on the quay belong to someone. Lift one within 12 m of its owner: they shout, remember it (weight 6), trust drops by 1, Fientje hears. Put it back within 15 s: they calm down. |
| Handovers | `runs.ts` | Deliver: the employer hands you the parcel (F). Cargo from the ship: call up at the gangway (F) and a sailor lowers it on a rope, one by one. |
| Goods system | `client/src/game/goods.ts` | Every liftable thing: lift the top one, set it down anywhere, stack up to three, or let it fall into the Schelde. Goods have heights you can stand on. |
| Movement | `firstPerson.ts`, `rijnkaai.ts` | Jump (about 0.55 m, onto a crate), crouch (eyes from 1.62 to 1.05 m, half speed), step onto low things, fall off edges. No jumping while carrying. |
