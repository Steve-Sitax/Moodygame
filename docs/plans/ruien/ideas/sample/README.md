# Rui and junction room samples

Six generated ideas for [issue #272](https://github.com/Steve-Sitax/Moodygame/issues/272). Pick one R and one J before building the playable sample. These are design pictures, not measured reconstructions or an environment acceptance pass. Build cost below means relative modelling, fitting and checking effort, not a price or a measured frame-time cost. [Prompts and revisions](prompts.md).

All rui options use class M: 3.6 m inside width, bed -4.9 m, water about -4.4 m, one 0.8 m walkway at -3.9 m, and a semicircular brick vault with spring -2.7 m and crown -0.9 m. Antigoon stands 3.85 m tall on the bed, leaving 0.15 m at the centre. People use the walkway. Do not copy the two walkways or deeper water of class G.

All room options use the 7.5 × 5.5 m footprint of plan room R1 by Sint-Pauluskerk: rectangle [153, 287.5, 160.5, 293] in [ruien-routes.json](../../ruien-routes.json), at the same bed and water levels. Picture R1 is a tunnel option, not that room's ID. The other seven room footprints differ; this sample must not resize them. Views compare roof construction and stair arrival; the native plan must set the actual mouth positions and connected banks.

## Rui

**[R1 — Open stone edge](R1.jpg).** A narrow unrailed cobbled path has worn bluestone edge blocks beneath a plain brick barrel and round light shaft. This costs least to build: one continuous paving strip and edge, with no rail fittings. Recommended for the repeated tunnel because its outline stays simple and the narrow walking route remains clear.

**[R2 — Iron edge rail](R2.jpg).** Plain iron posts and two rails guard the water edge, with mounting shoes on the vertical face to preserve the clear walking width. Cost is higher than R1: posts, joints, matching corrosion and collision need checking along bends. Choose this if a guarded path matters more than the open view of the channel.

**[R3 — Covered side-drain crossing](R3.jpg).** A deep egg-shaped brick drain feeds a recessed gutter under a flush iron grate across the path; a rectangular stone light well replaces the round shaft. Cost is medium: reuse the drain approach, but fit its ring, sill, grate and stain trail to the narrower walkway. Recommended as an occasional inlet variation, rather than repeating a drain in every bay.

## Junction room

**[J1 — Shallow brick dome](J1.jpg).** A broad shallow dome covers the meeting water, with a stair arriving through a corner arch and no column in the channel. Cost is high: the dome, corner transitions and tunnel openings need individually fitted brick courses and clearance checks. Choose this for a distinctive room; it is less convenient to adapt to the larger rectangular footprints.

**[J2 — Groin vaults on wall piers](J2.jpg).** Crossing brick vaults bear on piers against the walls, leaving the water open; the stair turns onto the cobbled landing behind the left wall. Cost is medium to high: vault intersections need careful fitting, but bays and stone bearing blocks can be reused. Recommended for the sample because the construction is readable and can extend to larger rooms without making the roof taller.

**[J3 — Iron beams and brick ceiling](J3.jpg).** Shallow brick arches between iron beams give the room a nearly flat roof, with a direct stair arrival and a short rail beside its landing. Cost is medium: repeatable beams simplify the roof, but their bearings, grate opening and undersides add parts. Choose this for a more industrial room; beam undersides must retain the full 4 m clearance above the bed.

## Picture review and build limits

The saved pictures were checked for a single dry walkway in each tunnel, readable material scale, worn edges, enclosed oil lamps, solid masonry and dry bluestone stairs. R1 was revised to remove a blind end and change its lamp. All first room drafts were rejected for low tunnel mouths and insufficient exposed walkway face; revised views restore taller mouths and the visible drop to water. J2 and J3 also lost isolated inner-bank paths; J3's unwanted lamp supply pipes were removed. No pipe is proposed by this set.

Perspective cannot certify the 0.15 m giant clearance, shaft depth, room footprint or complete stair route. Those remain measured build checks, including Antigoon's body width under the curved roof, a continuous human route between mouths, the full stair to street, and every beam and throat. Keep cobble rows aligned through turns with raised seam stones; fit stairs and kerbs without gaps. Check all sides and undersides in native daylight, rain and darkness before any repetition across the network. No native geometry or placement was changed here.

JPEGs are 1600 px wide with their original aspect ratios and each below 600 kB. Built-in image generation; PhotoCraft resize and JPEG export.
