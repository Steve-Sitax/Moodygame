Build constraints: gallery 6 m wide; central channel 3 m; cobbled ledges 1.5 m each at −3.6 m; vault crown −0.9 m; channel bed −6.1 m; usual water −4.6 m. Drain openings: about 1 × 1.6 m. The written dimensions govern; picture perspective is illustrative.

## Before modelling

- Each A option places the stair volume behind the gallery wall and uses stone treads. Fit the full 3.6 m descent, turns and headroom in a measured section before choosing its footprint.
- A1 requires a verified closed-lid fit and lifting/stay mechanism. A2 shows the street door closed; its concealed stair turn still needs a measured section.
- B1 and B3 show shallow open runnels across the ledge; B2 provides the clearest covered crossing. The B3 grille needs a maintenance reason and a clear swing.
- Keep the C arches outside the required centre-channel clearance. Background hall layouts, distant cascades and repeated fittings are atmosphere, not proposed additions to the network.
- D3 is an offset access-bay treatment; its reach and climb-out clearance need a section. D1 and D2 are the principal hall daylight options.
- Retain modest material-specific edge wear. The chosen scheme still needs measured geometry, proper lamp selection, and native daylight/rain/night checks; these pictures are not an environment acceptance pass.
