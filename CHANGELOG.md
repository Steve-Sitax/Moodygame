## [Unreleased]

- The draught horses are made again in far more detail: a real head with jowls, flared nostrils and lips, cupped ears, a rounded chest, a smooth stuffed collar and round rings, and a hand-painted coat (bay, chestnut, black, or a brown roan that no longer looks pink) with hair that lies the way it grows, dark legs, blazes, mud on the feathers and worn leather and brass on the harness. At the walk they nod their heads, their ears turn now and then, and their tails swish at the flies. Dray horses have their traces hooked to the shafts. The farrier's horse and the Rijnkaai's waiting dray horse are the same horse now.
- The Kempen pig is a real pig now: a deep, heavy body on short strong legs, big round hams and shoulders, a big head with heavy jowls and lop ears over its eyes, uneven skin folds, pale bristles and caked mud. The cart dog has the broad, deep muzzle and hanging lips of a Belgian draught dog.
- Dogs, cats and pigs set off and stop without their paws sliding over the stones: the paws stay planted through the start, the change of gait and the stop, where they step into place.
