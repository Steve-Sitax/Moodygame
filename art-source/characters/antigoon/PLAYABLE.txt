ANTIGOON — PLAYABLE CHARACTER

Run from the repository:
  powershell -File native-godot/scripts/play-antigoon.ps1
The character test uses no saved town and changes no player progress.
WASD: move. Shift: run. Mouse: look. Left click: strike. Escape: release mouse.
L: toggle light refuge. T: day/night. R: reset. 1/2/3: smirk/laugh/angry.

Reusable visual scene: native-godot/assets/characters/antigoon/antigoon.tscn
Skinned model: native-godot/assets/characters/antigoon/antigoon-playable.glb
Editable rig: art-source/characters/antigoon/source/antigoon-playable.blend
Restore the editable source with pack_delivery.py unpack (see below).
Original approved v13 sources are retained separately.

The model is 5.4 metres tall and 563,375 triangles, with 46 bones, five facial morph controls,
separate eyes/irises, teeth and mouth cavity. Body/head maps are 4096 pixels;
belt maps are 2048 pixels. Iris/eyelid albedo maps are 1024 pixels.
Wrist skin uses its own material to prevent cloth texture bleed.
Albedo, roughness and tangent normals are embedded
in the GLB. Godot generates distance LODs when creating the actor.
Animation clips: idle, walk, run, attack, throw, hit, recoil_light, death, roar,
smirk, laugh and angry. Root translation belongs to the gameplay brain.

Combat: 120 health, 15 damage per player strike with a 0.8 second cooldown.
Run speed: 4.8 m/s versus the player's 3.4. Walk: 1.8 m/s.
Kick wind-up: 0.62 seconds; full attack: 1.35 seconds; range: 2.2 metres.
The host validates range, walls, light and target health at contact.
Street lamps and working carried lanterns protect players. Rain/wet lantern
rules use the existing native service. Daylight makes him return home.

Native town: automatic night event, 20:00–06:00, in single and multiplayer.
F6 remains a separate single-player investigation opt-in.
Spawns require a dark, connected back alley, giant clearance and no player
line of sight. Antigoon patrols connected street routes.
He throws stones at burning lamps from darkness. A 0.7 s wind-up precedes
flight; impact extinguishes the lamp until 20:00 the following night.
Outages persist across midnight and saves; normal lamplighting cannot
override the outage. Existing lamp geometry and placement are preserved.
Nearby visible outdoor residents scream and flee along paths to their homes.
He occasionally chases one. Light, lost sight or house entry ends pursuit.
A caught resident falls, waits at least 3 s and until Antigoon is over 14 m
away, gets up, runs home, rests inside 8 s and resumes their normal schedule.
The event temporarily owns resident poses on all clients. No permanent
resident health loss. Active transport and existing actor actions are not
preempted. Alarm cries are original synthetic voices.
Multiplayer uses one host-owned enemy and disables the investigation route.
Health/position persist in world_state; save/load resets the live simulation.
Use --no-antigoon to disable the client encounter part during other tests.

Verification:
  dotnet run --project native-godot/services/proofs/antigoon/Antigoon.Proof.csproj
  powershell -File native-godot/scripts/play-antigoon.ps1 -Proof
Godot proof loads the actual GLB, checks animations and runs service checks
against an in-memory database. Logs and Blender/Godot renders are in logs/
and review/. This test arena is a functional lab, not finished city artwork.

Limits: the full authored Ruien map and branching quest campaign are not
included. Native street placement is conservative and can refuse to spawn
when no giant-sized dark route exists. A live two-client town playthrough
still needs acceptance. Animation is authored in place without terrain IK
or cloth simulation; hair is sculpted geometry. The roar clip has no voice
performance. Footsteps/impact reuse the project's Kenney CC0 sounds;
heartbeat is original runtime synthesis.

Street verification: 19 brain + 17 event + 25 host checks. Native street
preview: review/night-event-proof-06/antigoon-street-event.png. The staged
proof checks an authored alley, a giant-sized route out, actual resident
panic and rendering. Character maps use native sky/lamp light and fog.
Resident fleeing uses accelerated existing crowd strides; knockdown/rise
are procedural poses, not ragdolls or dedicated motion-capture clips.

Repository delivery: lossless 24 MiB chunks in native-godot/assets/characters/
antigoon/packed and art-source/characters/antigoon/source/packed. The game
loads its runtime bundle directly, including from an exported PCK. Restore
the standalone GLB and editable Blender source with:
  python art-source/characters/antigoon/pack_delivery.py unpack
After editing/exporting both files, run the same command with pack.
Hashes verify exact model, embedded textures and source reconstruction.

Selectable encounter: F8 > Events > Event: Antigoon (development mode).
It starts a fresh giant at 22:00 and puts Jef at a validated alley entrance.
Close the panel to let the encounter run. Console: event antigoon.
Automatic encounters remain enabled every night 20:00-06:00. Defeating
Antigoon persists in the save; the dev button can restart him for testing.
