# Carolus capital ornaments: partial repair

`scripts/carve-carolus-capitals.py` replaces 32 identified leaf bars and 12 Ionic
discs in `r001009`; its candidate preserves all other original vertex rows and
material `r001008`. The v6 candidate is installed as a partial ornament repair.

The revised leaves have three paired branching lobes with split tapered leaflets,
deep silhouette cut-ins, a raised midrib and six incised vein channels. Their
roots enter the retained bell; only the terminal spear bends outward. A 32 mm
blade tapers through a rounded perimeter of roughly 5 mm radius. Interior stone
more than 2 mm from the silhouette stays at least 10 mm thick; no face consists
only of coincident front/back boundary vertices. Eight leaves fit separate angular
sectors around each bell. Volute geometry remains unchanged from the v4 review.
Its back is a mating face; the visible rim retains its 6 mm arris and rounded ends.

Explicit charts survive triangulation. The actual curved leaf front and back are
angle-unwrapped and optimized for minimum stretch, with seams only along their
shared perimeter. Each chart is normalized by surface area to the source's 3 m
repeat; no geometric or normal changes accompany the unwrap. Volute charts remain
unchanged. Islands fit within one mortar-free block in `carolus_bluestone.jpg`;
no image is modified.
The generator checks manifold solids, winding, noncollapsed UVs and internal
chart continuity. Exported float rows must also pass preservation checks.

Native review covers close, side, back, underside and targeted root/tip views in
day, rain and night: 72 captures and 16 passing state checks. The broad stretched
bands around leaf edges are reduced; local distortion remains. Night views limit
fine-detail inspection. Readback keeps positions and UVs exact, all 32,813 retained
triangles' colours exact, and collision/support unchanged. Normal encoding differs
by at most 0.000114; new colours follow the engine's exact 8-bit packing.

Retained faceted columns/bells/caps, coarse and repeated materials, block courses
on carved bodies, sharp adjacent stone and unfinished statues remain unresolved.
Connected drainage is unverified. These defects block overall capital, church
and environment acceptance; the ornament improvement does not exempt them.
